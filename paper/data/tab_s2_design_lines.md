# T4 — Simulation conditions (Study 2)

| | Persona design | Decision process | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | | *answers the task page by page* | |
| Benchmark · simple | attribute list: age, gender, industry, position, stance | follows a fixed script | 42 personas × 5 runs |
| Benchmark · layered | attribute list + an MBTI-based "thinking style" paragraph + the archetype's business background | same | same 42 personas × 5 runs |
| Roleplay | biography: a randomly drawn fact card (demographics, career, household, consumption habits + 8 personality traits) written into a short life story by a writer who does not know the task; the agent reads only the story | reads and answers like a student; decides itself how long to deliberate | 42 personas × 5 runs |
| **Team (5 members)** | | *individual proposals, then group discussion, then submission* | |
| Benchmark · simple | as individual simple | fixed speaking order (7 turns per segment); discussion length set by the program | 42 teams × 5 runs |
| Benchmark · layered | as individual layered; same 42 teams | same | 42 teams × 5 runs |
| Roleplay | as individual roleplay | round 1: open discussion, whoever wants to speak speaks; round 2: each member first takes a private position, then the team discusses until it converges on one plan; length not preset | 42 teams × 5 runs |

Note. Benchmark and roleplay differ in two things only: how the persona is written (attribute list vs biography) and how the discussion ends (set by the program vs by the agents). Same model and temperature throughout. Human reference: 16 EMBA teams from the same classroom task.
